The History of Maison Jourdan

Interview with Mr. Frédéric Jourdan, Director

Can you give us an overview of the history of Maison Jourdan?

In 1989, I took over, with the help of my wife, the small body shop that my father owned. It was in 1993 that we founded Jourdan S.A. and acquired the IVECO dealership. Then, 5 years later, the purchase of Zimmer Machinery took place in Saint Mard in Belgium, the area was then transformed to reach 7000m² and we are, since that day, 17 people on the Virton site.

The opening of Jourdan s.p.r.l. took place in 2004. This transport and towing company currently employs 12 people and specializes in concrete transport, exceptional convoy and heavy truck towing throughout Europe. 5 years passed before the acquisition of the NRI SA quarry in Etalle whose main activities are recycling and extraction of natural materials.

Finally, in August 2012, we opened the second IVECO dealership in Grand-Duchy of Luxembourg which has 9 people.

Can you give us some details about the Luxembourg dealership?

When the former Iveco dealer decided to cease his activity, the factory did not wish to lose its market share, so it asked us to take over distribution in Luxembourg. After a year and a half of searching for a building, we were able to take advantage of the infrastructure of the HOYER lux group. The area is 2000m² with 800m² of offices and two and a half hectares of parking.

What is the activity of the Jourdan garage in Kehlen?

Different departments are available there. Indeed, the first is dedicated to the sale of new IVECO and Max Trailer vehicles (Faymonville trailers). There is also an Iveco used vehicles service, which gives us the title of "Iveco Used Trucks" for the brand in Benelux.

In addition to the garage activity, we have a tachograph service, speed limiter and a Luxembourg technical inspection line for Utility and Heavy Goods Vehicles.

Do you offer assembly services?

The qualified personnel of the Jourdan garage allows mounting, on a utility vehicle or a heavy truck, of tail lifts, night heaters, air conditioning, tires, aluminum rims, spot lights and many other accessories. Our technicians possess all the necessary knowledge to work on tankers transporting petroleum and chemical products.

What services are available regarding vehicle outfitting?

Compared to our competitors, our strength is the complete knowledge of the fitting out of a vehicle. Therefore, a finished product can be delivered on request. Indeed, it can be a workshop vehicle, a shop vehicle, a moving truck, a refrigerated box, etc... Moreover, thanks to our partners, we are recognized as a European bodybuilder-constructor (COC, COP).

What about breakdown service?

We strive to meet customer needs. For this, a vehicle towing and customer mobility system is planned. We also perform repairs directly on the road, which represents 90% of our interventions. Accidents are also our priority and we do everything so that the customer has only one contact person and only one "emergency" phone number to dial.
